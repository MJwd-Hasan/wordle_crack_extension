Wordle Guesser

Local Solver · Version 2.0.1

A local Wordle assistant that narrows the possible-answer list after every submitted guess.

What it does

  • Applies every green, yellow, and gray tile to the answer list.
  • Handles repeated letters using Wordle’s two-pass feedback rules.
  • Shows matching possible answers and letter frequencies.
  • Checks candidates against the complete submitted guess history.
Open NYT Wordle or Wordle Archive. The analysis panel updates after each guess is evaluated. The extension never enters guesses for you.

Everything runs locally in your browser. No board data is sent anywhere.

The bundled vocabulary is broad and may not exactly match every game's official answer list.
